## The one-liner

**Wanderdart — throw a dart at a map of America and let it decide where you go next.**

## Elevator pitch (2–3 sentences)

Wanderdart turns "where should we even go?" into a single tap. You throw a comic dart at a map of the United States, and it sticks on a real, hand-picked destination — complete with the reason it's worth the trip and a wisecracking host who's not impressed that you needed an app to decide. Go there for real, log it, and watch your miles, states, and cities add up across 50 achievements.

## Short description (App Store style, ~50 words)

Can't decide where to travel? Let a dart decide. Throw it at a map of America and Wanderdart pins a real place from 1,300+ hand-picked spots — beaches, ghost towns, diners, national parks, Route 66 and more — with a reason to go and a host with attitude. Log your trips, collect achievements, share the postcard.

## Long description

Wanderdart is a travel-roulette app with a personality. Instead of endless scrolling and "I don't know, what do *you* want to do?", you throw a single dart at a map of the United States. It lands on one of 1,300+ real, curated destinations across 13 categories, tells you what it is and why it's worth the drive, and hands you a one-liner from **Doc Hank**, a grumpy host who treats hesitation as a personal insult.

The twist that makes it stick: it's a game, not just a randomizer. When you actually make the trip, you "Log it" — and Wanderdart builds a **travel log** of the miles you've covered and the states and cities you've reached, feeding **50 achievements** from "First Dart" to "Around the World," "Conquer 50 States," and "Complete Route 66." Every result exports as a shareable postcard, so a single throw is ready for Instagram, TikTok, or the group chat.

No account, no tracking, and it works offline. Open it, throw, go.

## Key features

- **Throw-a-dart discovery** — one tap picks your next destination; no decision paralysis.
- **1,300+ real, hand-picked places** across 13 categories: national parks, beaches, lighthouses, roadside attractions, small towns, scenic drives, dark-sky sites, diners, hot springs, ghost towns, drive-ins, Route 66, and music heritage.
- **A host with attitude** — Doc Hank delivers the verdict with a comic one-liner.
- **Travel log** — miles traveled, states and cities visited, tracked from the places you actually go.
- **50 achievements** — milestones, region completions, category collections, and special routes.
- **Instant share** — every result becomes a postcard-style image for social.
- **Offline & account-free** — no sign-up, no signal required, no data harvesting.

## Reviewer quick-start (try it in 60 seconds)

1. Open the app and tap **Throw**.
2. Watch the dart hit the map and read your destination card (place, category, reason).
3. Tap **Hank Says** for another one-liner; tap **Open Maps** to see where it is.
4. Tap **★ Log it**, add a star rating, and hit **Save** — that place now counts toward your travel log and achievements.
5. Tap **🧭 (Travel Log)** to see miles, states, cities, and achievement progress.
6. On any result, tap **Share** to generate the postcard image.
