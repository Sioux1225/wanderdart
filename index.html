The whole idea

Three taps between bored and gone.

01

Throw the dart

Tap once. A dart flies at the United States and sticks somewhere you didn't choose — that's the point.

02

Meet your place

A real destination lands on the table: the state, the spot, its category, and a straight-talking reason it's worth the trip.

03

Log it & collect

Actually go? Log it. Rack up miles, states, and cities, and chip away at 50 achievements.
